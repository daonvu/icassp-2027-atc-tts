# Synthetic Voices, Unverified Instructions
## Speech Synthesis and Air Traffic Control Security

**Dao Vu and Peng Wei**  
Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering  
George Washington University

Companion site for an ICASSP 2027 submission.

## About the study

This study compares off-the-shelf Chatterbox speech synthesis with air traffic control (ATC) fine-tuning. We examine how reference duration and adaptation affect word recovery, speech timing, speaker resemblance, and predicted naturalness.

The aviation question is whether accessible speech synthesis warrants attention as a source of plausible unauthorized controller instructions. The study measures properties of recorded and synthesized speech. Listener recognition, pilot acceptance, over-the-air transmission, and correction timing remain unmeasured.
